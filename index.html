Subsidiary Journals: Cash Receipts (CRJ), Cash Payments (CPJ), Petty Cash (PCJ)

Quick Reference: Common Accounts & Rules (Click to expand)

Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ)

Details: Write Sales for cash sales; write the customer/payer name for other receipts (e.g. C Hopkins, B Blue).

Sundry Details accounts: Capital, Rent income, Interest income, Commission received, Drawings.

Rule: Analysis of receipts = Sales + Trade receivables control + Sundry Amount

Cash Payments Journal (CPJ)

Payee: Write the person or vendor paid (e.g. Waltons, AA Traders, Cash for wages/float).

Sundry Details accounts: Furniture, Equipment, Vehicle, Drawings, Wages, Rent expense, Stationery, Repairs, Trading stock.

Rule: Bank = Wages + Purchases + Trade payables control + Sundry Amount

Petty Cash Journal (PCJ)

Details: Write the specific expense item (e.g. Stamps, Cleaning, Envelopes, Milk and coffee).

Sundry Details accounts: Refreshments, Drawings, Cleaning, Donations, Petrol, Repairs, Sundry expenses.

Rule: Petty cash/total = Wages + Postage + Stationery + Sundry Amount
